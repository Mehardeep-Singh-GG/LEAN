## 1. Formal Mathematical Hypotheses

### Prediction P_M8_1 (P4): Real-LLM Fact Survival Monotonicity
Under progressive compression ladders (16-bit FP $\to$ 8-bit RTN $\to$ 4-bit RTN $\to$ 3-bit RTN $\to$ 2-bit RTN) on real pretrained language models (EleutherAI Pythia-70M), individual factual associations are discarded in reverse order of pretraining frequency in The Pile ($p_{\text{Pile}}$).
When evaluated at the critical degradation boundary ($B \le B_{1/2}^{\text{fact}}$), the Area Under the Receiver Operating Characteristic Curve (ROC-AUC) predicting fact survival from Pile pretraining frequency alone satisfies:
$$\text{AUC}(p_{\text{Pile}} \to \text{Survival}) \ge 0.75$$

### Prediction P_M8_2 (P3): Knapsack Scaling with Pile Zipf Exponent
The distribution of factual entity-relation occurrences in The Pile pretraining corpus follows a Zipf power law with empirical exponent $\alpha_{\text{Pile}}$:
$$p_i \propto i^{-(1+\alpha_{\text{Pile}})}$$
Under continuous knapsack allocation, the empirical excess loss power-law exponent $\gamma$ satisfies:
$$\gamma \approx \alpha_{\text{Pile}}$$
specifically matching $\alpha_{\text{Pile}}$ within the empirical 95% bootstrap confidence interval $[\gamma_{\text{low}}, \gamma_{\text{high}}]$ or within 25% relative error.

### Prediction P_M8_3 (P5): Asymmetric Half-Life Ratio
In-context procedural skills (e.g., key-value lookup, sequence copy, sequence reversal, pattern induction) have compact algorithmic representations ($c_{\text{alg}} K(s)$) and high corpus usage frequency compared to individual factual associations.
Consequently, across the progressive compression ladder, procedural reasoning skills survive to significantly tighter bit budgets than factual associations. Defining $B_{1/2}(X)$ as the bit budget where capability $X$ drops to 50% of its uncompressed accuracy:
$$\frac{B_{1/2}^{\text{fact}}}{B_{1/2}^{\text{skill}}} \ge 2.0\times$$

### Prediction P_M8_4: Bounded Core Saturation Slope
The physical description length of the algorithmic reasoning core ($\text{DL}_{\text{skill}}$) saturates across model scale. The scaling exponent of procedural description length with respect to model parameter count $N$ satisfies:
$$\frac{\partial \ln \text{DL}_{\text{skill}}}{\partial \ln N} \le 0.30$$

### Prediction P_M8_5: Counterfactual Fact-Swap Orthogonality
Ablating or perturbing factual associative memory representations in MLP feed-forward down-projection layers selectively degrades factual knowledge retrieval without compromising procedural reasoning execution circuits:
$$\Delta_{\text{skill}} = | \text{Acc}_{\text{skill}}^{\text{base}} - \text{Acc}_{\text{skill}}^{\text{perturbed}} | \le 0.05 \quad (\le 5\%)$$
