Milestone M10B evaluates the cross-architecture generalization of the **Priced-Quanta Equation v1** on all locally feasible pretrained models ($\le 500\text{M}$ parameters) on Apple Silicon M2 (8.0 GB Unified Memory).

### 1.2 Apple Silicon Resource Safeguards:
- **Memory Guard:** Peak resident MPS memory must not exceed **$3.5\text{ GB}$** at any time. Models are evaluated sequentially with layer-wise stream quantization and explicit garbage collection (`gc.collect()`, `torch.mps.empty_cache()`).
- **Disk Space Guard:** Available disk space is strictly monitored (starting budget $\approx 9.0\text{ GB}$). A safety floor of $\ge 2.5\text{ GB}$ free disk space is maintained by streaming checkpoints sequentially and clearing transient cache if headroom falls below $3.0\text{ GB}$.

## 3. Evaluation Protocol

1. **Ruler A (Layer-wise Progressive Quantization):**
   - Symmetric group Round-to-Nearest (RTN, group size $g=128$) across bit budgets:
     $$b \in \{16\text{ (FP16)}, 8, 4, 3, 2\}\text{ bits/param}$$
   - Embeddings and LM head preserved in native FP16 per standard LLM quantization practice.
2. **Probes Battery:**
   - **Factual Probes ($N=100$ per model):** Candidate-conditioned cloze accuracy evaluated across pretraining frequency deciles.
   - **Procedural Skill Probes ($N=50$ per model):** In-context algorithmic induction, syntax bracket matching, and sequence manipulation tasks.
3. **P6 Pareto Test:**
   - Direct head-to-head comparison of larger compressed model against uncompressed smaller baseline at equivalent total physical bit capacity:
     - `Pythia-410M` (2-bit RTN $\approx 810\text{ Mb}$) vs `Pythia-70M` (FP16 $\approx 1,126\text{ Mb}$).
     - `SmolLM2-360M` (2-bit RTN $\approx 720\text{ Mb}$) vs `gpt2` (FP16 $\approx 1,984\text{ Mb}$).

1. **Criterion C1 (Prediction Interval Coverage):**

   Empirical half-life ratio satisfies $B_{1/2}^{\text{fact}} / B_{1/2}^{\text{skill}} \ge 2.0\times$ across every tested model architecture.

   Cross-model parameter scaling regression slope satisfies $\partial \ln \text{DL}/\partial \ln N \le 0.150$.

5. **Criterion C5 (Cross-Scale Pareto Advantage):**
