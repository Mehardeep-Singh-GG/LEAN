# Brand & Engineering Identity

## Name and What We Do
**Project LEAN / Llama-AirGap** (by Mehardeep Singh & Gurarpit Singh) — hardware-verified systems & AI engineering that runs 7B–8B open-weight models in **3.84 GiB of RAM** on standard 8 GB laptops with **88.0% accuracy (7B)**, **94.0% ± 0.0% accuracy (3B)**, and **75.0% standalone pure deductive logic**.

## Audience
Hackathon judges, systems engineers, AI researchers, and developers who value real hardware metrics, physical intuition, and high-craft visual storytelling over buzzwords.

## How Results Must Always Be Presented
1. **Real Numbers Only:** Every claim is backed by exact, measured figures (`15.23 GB → 3.84 GiB`, `88.0% vs 12.0%`, `Cliff's δ = 1.000`, `14–22 words/sec`, `4.08 GB free RAM headroom`, `0.2 ms` lookup). Never invent or round away real experimental results.
2. **Plain Physical Intuition First:** Explain complex math (like Mori-Zwanzig projection or mixed-precision quantization) in tangible, everyday language first ("20% Thinking Engine vs. 80% Trivia Warehouse") before showing symbols.
